About Alberto

Alberto is a singer-songwriter and producer known for his soulful vocals, intimate lyricism, and a sound that moves fluidly between R&B, jazz, and contemporary pop. Born and raised in Los Angeles, he grew up surrounded by music — from his grandmother's vinyl collection to late-night jazz sessions in the city's underground clubs.

His debut album Love Moods introduced audiences to a voice both deeply personal and universally resonant. Songs drawn from real experience — loss, longing, connection, and joy — earned him a devoted following and critical acclaim across the globe.

With Love Moods II, Alberto pushes further, weaving orchestral arrangements with modern production to craft something timeless yet immediate. At the heart of it all is a simple passion: creating music that moves people.
